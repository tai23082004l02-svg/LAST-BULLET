# Campaign & Summon — v0.8.0

Gameplay is now auto-fire. Drag anywhere except buttons to move; use the robot skill to survive. Deflect, permanent bullets, Perfect and return-strike boss mechanics from older guides are retired.

## Campaign
Five stages, each with three timed combat waves and a boss. Upgrades carry across waves within one run; retry resets run upgrades. Boss arrival times remain 90/105/120/120/135 seconds.

1. Training Bay / Sentinel: telegraphed charge and three-shot fan.
2. Reflection Works / Mirror Warden: two cover pillars, alternating 3-second shield phases, five-shot fan. Move around shield or attack while down.
3. Energy Furnace / Furnace Heart: warning tiles turn hot, ground marks and wide fan.
4. Magnetic Station / Polaris: marked danger lanes, targeted ground strikes and fan volleys; no single-bullet steering objective.
5. Machine Core / Core Prime: cover, hot tiles, ground strikes; below half HP adds charges and a seven-shot fan. No capture or returned bullet required.

Clear opens the next stage. First clear gives 3 tickets, repeat clear 1; gold adds 40+20*stage_index on top of run/mission rewards. Stars: clear; HP>=3; victory by boss-arrival-time+60s. Replay keeps the highest stars. Failure cannot unlock/clear a stage. Existing clears/stars and robot ownership stay intact.

## Summon
Sảnh → Summon. Pull x1 or x10 with tickets. Base rates before pity: Rare 75%, Epic 20%, Legendary 5%. Rare pool Volt/Cobalt/Scout; Epic Frost/Ember; Legendary Nova. Aegis is the free starter. Each robot now has its own weapon and active skill; see README.

After 9 consecutive Rare pulls, the next pull is Epic or Legendary. After 49 non-Legendary pulls, the next is Legendary. Legendary resets both counters; Epic resets Epic+ only. Duplicate rewards: 5/15/50 shards by tier. Target exchanges cost Rare 30, Epic 90, Legendary 300. Already-owned exchange is rejected.

Tickets: five for a genuinely new profile (not an update gift); +1 per completed mission, +1 at 120s and 30 kills once/run, +1 for finished Blitz, campaign clear rewards. Missions now use kills, skill uses and kill-chain. Summon commits inventory/receipt before reveal; skip/review cannot award again. Admin uses its separate free inventory/profile. No ads or paid pulls.
